# Audit 2026-11-10: physics, node behaviour, performance, usability

Asked for: performance opportunities, usability, that all nodes behave correctly, the tests, a hands-on
pass through the editor (hiding and showing nodes, changing settings, adding, removing and reconnecting
nodes), and a check that the physics calculations are correct.

Done by four agents (physics, performance, two for the nodes: every Python run on the CPU, in-process,
small parts, under a memory kill switch) and one hands-on session in the test editor (port 8766, GPU).
Nothing in the code was changed. **Checked again** = re-run or read in the code by the coordinator after
the agent reported it; **agent** = measured by the agent only.

Not covered: the lamp recipes and other big parts, `fine` FEA detail on the CPU, Frame Optimization's
layout beyond two small cases, Join Frames / Connect to with optimized frames, worker pools with several
workers in the headless runs, picking faces with real mouse clicks (the browser pane was hidden: menus,
buttons and fields were driven through the page, wires through the store's own connect / reconnect).

## In short

- **Physics: the maths is right.** Element, load kinds, load cases, beam element, section properties,
  buckling formulas, the truss LP and topology's sensitivities all match closed-form answers, most to
  0.0x%. What is off is how results are *read off the voxel grid*: the headline "max stress / safety
  factor" moves about ±10% with the detail level, and thin walls at draft come out a few % too stiff.
- **Nodes: no new wrong-result bug in the old nodes; two in the new ones.** Offset Faces leaves stray
  slivers and a loose speck at some voxel sizes, and Boolean Union with a rounded or chamfered edge
  swells faces the two bodies share. Five P1 items of the 2026-10-20 audit are still open.
- **Performance: the engine's planning and caching overhead is already negligible** (a no-op re-run is
  1-5 ms). The clearest gains are in the editor: the 3D view redraws ~120 times a second while nothing
  changes, and every progress event re-renders the whole 3D view.
- **Usability: the main flows work** (new graph, library, wiring, bypass, groups, undo, face menus,
  save, export, static analysis, topology and frame optimization, variants, section). A list of small
  rough edges is below.
- **Tests:** 591 passed, 0 failed (CPU suite, 14 min, peak 2.4 GB); all 42 GPU-vs-CPU tests passed.
  `test_recipes_are_valid` fails on `recipes/testlamp2.json` (tracked: two unwired nodes) and on the
  untracked work-in-progress `kakformsgrej.json`.

### Findings

**F1. The headline "max stress" and "safety factor" come from almost-empty surface elements.**
`engine/fea/analysis.py`: `element_stresses` keeps every element with any material (`phi > 0`, from 2%
full), and the report takes the maximum over them.
- Agent: round bar in torsion, true 17.64 MPa: reported 20.2 (draft), 20.7 (medium), 21.3, 21.6: +14 to
  +22%, and it does not shrink with finer detail. The maximum sits in elements 4-7% full whose centres
  are outside the bar. Elements at least half full give 17.26 / 17.63 MPa (-2.2 / -0.06%).
- Checked again, bracket sample in the editor: safety factor 3.52 / 3.88 / 3.47 at draft / medium /
  fine (stress 12.8 / 11.6 / 13.0 MPa) while the deflection settles (4.21 / 4.33 / 4.35 mm).
- Checked again, plates (60×20×3, 60×20×2.3, 80×30×4) at the normal detail levels: reported stress
  -2..-11% against Mc/I, not monotonic in the detail.
- Also feeds Topology's and Frame's body check (`nodes_fea.judge`), so a frame is thickened to a number
  with this noise in it.
- Fix idea: take the report's maximum over elements at least half full, or from the nodal field that is
  drawn (which is within 3-5%).

**F2. Walls whose faces fall inside elements are too stiff; nothing warns.** `model.element_fill` fills
an element from the distance at its centre and the stiffness is `phi × E`: a part-filled layer is smeared
over the whole element (too long a lever arm), and convex edges are over-filled.
- Agent, worst case (element size forced, faces half an element off on both sides): 10×10 bar -19 / -5.7
  / -1.5% deflection at 5 / 10 / 20 elements across, stress low by the same; a 3 mm plate 3 elements
  thick -24.9%; a bar turned 45°, 8 elements across, -8.4%.
- Checked again at the editor's own detail levels (grid from the part's corner): the three plates are
  5.5-6.5 elements thick at draft and come out -2.9..-4.7% in deflection; medium and fine (10-19 elements)
  within 0.5%. So medium is fine for walls of 10+ elements; draft on thin-walled parts is a few % stiff,
  and much more when a wall is only 3-4 elements thick.
- The existing note ("the part is thin compared with the elements") needs more than half the elements
  part-filled; the 3-element plate has exactly half and gets none.
- Fix idea: warn from the thinnest wall in elements (under ~8), and fill edge elements by sub-sampling.

**F3. A bearing load not square to the hole's axis applies more force than typed** (agent).
`engine/fea/model.py` (`_bearing_norm`): 200 N at 45° to the axis applies 282.8 N radially (the radial
part of that force is 141 N); 18° off gives 210.8 N. `resultant()` (the arrow and the Offset's moment)
uses a third vector. Refused only above 3×. No note reaches the user.

**F4. "Max deflection" includes nodes outside the part** (agent): round bar in torsion +5.5% (medium) to
+14.7%; negligible in bending.

**F5. The frame's reported buckling factor uses one beam element per piece** (agent): a column fixed at
the foot and propped at the top gives 164.7 N against 114.2 N exact (+44%); two elements give +0.02%.
Sizing itself stays safe (each compressed bar is also checked with pinned Euler / Johnson, verified
exact); only the reported factor and the `buckling < safety` trigger are optimistic.

Smaller: `nodes_fea.py` calls `item.volume` / `item.is_watertight` on a kept CAD mesh (CLAUDE.md's rule
against trimesh's caches); the frame card's "g of bars" sums bars without their overlaps (agent: 14.7 g
on a block that weighs 7.1 g). Unconfirmed: a 0.6 mm gyroid sheet ~6% thinner than typed; cubic struts
at density 0.3 came out 0.321.

## 2. Node behaviour

sharp Booleans, Smooth Union, Texture (= the Multiply / Offset chain, difference 0.0), Coordinate, 3D

Topology and Frame Optimization end to end, the evaluator (only downstream nodes recompute, bypass,
keep_going, warnings replayed on cached runs), 22 of 22 pattern presets, the small recipes.

## 3. Performance opportunities

Measured first (CPU, the editor's own `Session` in-process): planning the lamp's 50-node graph 2-4 ms; a
no-op re-run 1 ms (5 ms without `have`); a cached 144-item list through 10 mapped nodes 2.5 ms; packing a
view ~1 ms per MB. In the test editor (GPU): after typing a value the Draft view is on screen after
0.75 s (0.45 s of it the debounce), Medium at 1.1 s, Final (56M voxels, 105 blocks, 1.9M triangles) at
6.4 s. None of that planning needs work. Ranked by expected gain:

| # | Opportunity | Evidence | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The 3D view redraws every frame while nothing changes.** `Viewport.tsx`'s `<Canvas>` has no `frameloop="demand"` (and the code no `invalidate`). A multi-million-triangle Final view is drawn ~120 times a second on the card the engine computes on | Checked again: 361 animation frames and 5,968 draw calls in 3 s with an idle scene and no run | S-M (needs `invalidate()` where things are mutated in place: camera turns, section drag, box-cut uniforms) |
| 2 | **Progress events: one WebSocket frame and one React pass each, and the whole 3D view re-renders on each.** `core.py` emits `progress` per list item for every mapped node, also lazy ones (~30 µs each); `Viewport()` (and Toolbar) subscribe to the whole `status` map; `EngineNode` filters all edges per card per update | Agent: 1,440 progress events in < 0.1 s for a 144-item list through 10 nodes. ## 4. Usability (hands-on in the test editor)

Worked as expected: New, typing a STEP path, adding nodes from the library's search, wiring, reconnecting
a wire's end, hiding and showing nodes (CAD view, implicit view, field slice), delete and
"delete and reconnect", undo / redo (also by keyboard), bypass, group / edit group / ungroup, Tidy, the
face menu (*Holes ▸ Round holes*: one edit, toast with Undo, quick-edit box), Save as / Save, Export (STL
reloaded: closed, one body), clear errors for a voxel of 0 or below, decimal commas, static analysis with
its card note and warnings, Optimize for topology (draft, 23 s, "Finished ✓") and frame (12 s, safety
factor 2.14), Section, Keep variant, Sliders, Print check.

Rough edges found:

1. **A new graph starts red.** New gives Import STEP with an empty path, so the first thing shown is
   "STEP file not found:" and Failed. A hint ("choose a STEP file") would be kinder than an error.
2. **A failing preview is run three times.** With Refine on, the same error is logged at Draft, Medium
   and Final when the failing node is all there is to show.
3. **The log line at the bottom keeps an old red error** after later runs succeed (a cached, successful
   preview adds no line), so it reads as if something were still wrong.
4. **New cards land on top of others.** Nodes added by a click in the library (525,106 then 565,146),
   a new group node, and the default starter graph (cards 320 apart, no gap) overlap or touch; shipped
   recipes laid out 300-320 apart overlap at today's card width.
5. **Text that is not a number stays in the field** ("abc", "1/2", empty) while the old value is used
   (the old audit's item, still there).
6. **Switching a Load's Kind keeps the Amount:** 100 N becomes 100 MPa or 100 N·m (safety factor 0.19),
   with only the unit label changing.
7. The quick-edit box clips its Edges buttons ("sharp round cha…").
8. Legend and card disagree slightly: displacement legend 0..4.95 mm, card note 4.99 mm.
9. The topology sample opens with the design space's CAD view and the un-optimized body both shown,
   coincident: a speckled surface (z-fighting).
10. After an optimization its warnings appear twice in the log (from the optimization, then from the
    preview that follows).
11. The selected node's help panel opens over the middle of the graph, also over the card whose
    Optimize was just clicked.
12. Group asks its name through the browser's own prompt box, unlike the editor's other dialogs.
13. Topology at draft raised Max thickness 4 → 5.85 mm; the closing note names 5.85 mm without saying it
    was raised.
